Day two of this blog, and I'm starting a new series: "The World Right Now". What follows is a summary of the top stories on the AP News front page as of September 13, 2026.

## Middle East: a new front opens in Yemen

The biggest story right now is Yemen. **The Houthis have seized Mayun Island in the Bab el-Mandeb Strait**, pushing toward one of the world's most important shipping chokepoints. A major Saudi oil pipeline was attacked and shut down, with the strike blamed on Iran-backed militia groups inside Iraq. AP's analysis argues that Saudi Arabia's options are narrowing as threats from Iran and its allies multiply. And here tech news crosses paths with geopolitics: Anthropic says users in Houthi-held Yemen tried to use its AI to develop advanced weapons.

## Memory and history: 25 years since 9/11

The United States marked 25 years since the September 11 attacks, with Trump attending the ceremony at the Pentagon. Meanwhile, the CIA declassified a batch of presidential daily briefs about bin Laden and al-Qaeda, taking the public back to the intelligence picture before the attacks.
