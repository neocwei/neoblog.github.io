**"Becoming"** is more than a name. It's a record of someone in the middle of becoming — learning, failing, rebuilding, and writing it down as it happens.

I came to programming from chemical engineering, and I learn in public: the wins, the bugs, the wrong turns that turned out to be shortcuts. You'll find those stories here, alongside digests of what's happening in the world outside my own head.

I don't have final answers to share. What I have is an honest notebook — and the hope that, page by page, the person writing it keeps getting closer to the person he's becoming.
